About

I started in IT in Harare, first at the City of Harare and then at FHI 360, where I ran the IT for offices across two provinces and kept more than 80 people connected, secure and backed up. That work taught me that data only matters when someone can act on it.

Graduate study in data analytics and visualization took me deep into graph data science: modelling networks in Neo4j, running algorithms like PageRank, Betweenness Centrality and Louvain, and presenting the results in dashboards people actually use.

I also hold a postgraduate diploma in sustainable project management, monitoring and evaluation, so I think about data products the way a product owner does: who uses it, what changes because of it, and how we measure that.

I'm looking for roles in data analytics, data product design and development, and product ownership.
